# Code of conduct

The project is a shared communications tool. Participants are expected to be
constructive, patient, and respectful, and to assume good intent.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate disruption, and unsolicited or non-consensual transmission
of regulated radio content. The operator is responsible for legal frequency,
bandwidth, power, antenna, and duty-cycle selection; that responsibility
extends to how this software is used.

Report concerns to the maintainers through the private reporting channel in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may remove contributions, close discussions, or revoke participation for
behavior that violates this code.
